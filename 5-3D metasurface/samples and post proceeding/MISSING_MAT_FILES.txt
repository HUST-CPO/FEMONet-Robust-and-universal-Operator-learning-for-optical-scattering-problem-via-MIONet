# Data directory: samples and post proceeding/mat_data/

## Why files are missing from the repository

3D COMSOL samples use **MATLAB v7.3 (HDF5)** `.mat` files that are typically **1–10+ GB** per full dataset (`Train_data_3Dcase3_521.mat`). Only the 261-sample `deepOnet`, `idx`, and FFT prediction files are kept when size allows.

## Files currently in mat_data

- `idx_3Dcase3_261.mat`
- `deepOnet_data_3Dcase3_261.mat`
- `E_train_pred_size_261_ddp_fft.mat`
- `E_test_pred_size_261_ddp_fft.mat`
